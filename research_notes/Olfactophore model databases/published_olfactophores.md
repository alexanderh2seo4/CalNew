# Published olfactophore / pharmacophore models for odor families in fragrance chemistry

Research notes, compiled 2026-09-28. Scope: every published olfactophore model (odor family, authors/year/venue, training set, method, features, validation, whether geometry/files are published, access/licence). Wiley (Helv Chim Acta, EurJOC, ChemBiodiv, Angew), ResearchGate and Perfumer & Flavorist full texts returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching. Facts from those papers therefore come from abstracts (via OpenAlex), from open-access papers that cite them, or from search-engine snippets, and each is labelled as such. Access status ("closed", "bronze", "gold", "diamond") is OpenAlex's `oa_status` field.

## Q1. Which olfactophore models exist? (catalogue across odor families)

### Takeaway
There are about 20 or more published olfactophore or SOR-geometry models. Most come from one lineage: Givaudan's Bajgrowicz/Fráter/Kraft group (Catalyst, later BIOVIA Discovery Studio; HipHop/HypoGen-type quantitative models trained on odor thresholds), which continued at Symrise after Kraft moved there. Most were published in paywalled Wiley journals, and nearly all are reported as a figure plus a short text description. The main families covered are muguet, marine, musk, sandalwood, ambergris, green/galbanum and rose (nitrile). Older "manual" or electron-topological models (Chastrette/Zakarya, Ohloff's triaxial rule, Bersuker/Gorbachov ETM, Ham & Jurs) cover sandalwood, ambergris, musk and camphoraceous odors.

### Inferences
- The only living quantitative olfactophore programme visible in the literature is Kraft's (Givaudan to 2020/21, then Symrise per recent affiliations). Its models (musk 2016, rose 2020, muguet 2023, ambergris 2025) are consistently built in Discovery Studio/Catalyst HypoGen-style and report correlation, cost and RMS metrics. Other groups (Riad/Phase; Golebiowski/Baldovini; RMIT) produced one-off models.
- Ohloff's triaxial rule, the Chastrette hypermotifs and the Bersuker/Gorbachov ETM "active fragments" are rule-based pharmacophores. They predate the pharmacophore-software era and have no machine-readable form.
- Older secondary summaries quote "hydrophobes at 5.5, 6.7 and 8.0 Å from the HBA" for earlier musk models, and "OH-to-quaternary-C ≈ 7.1 Å (pattern I) and 6.5 Å (pattern II)" for sandalwood. These appeared only in search-engine summaries whose primary sources I could not confirm, so they are **not** in the table (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- The original venue of the **1999 bifunctional muguet olfactophore** (hydroxycitronellal/Lyral; "Pelzer fragments") was not found. It may be in Kraft et al. 2000 *Angew* or an internal Givaudan/ACS symposium chapter. Unverified.
- **Bajgrowicz & Broger, "Molecular modelling in the design of new odorants; scope and limitations"** (Catalyst) was mentioned in a search summary but its venue/year was not confirmed. It is likely a chapter in a Givaudan/RSC symposium volume c. 1995–1999.
- **Rossiter KJ, "Structure−Odor Relationships", Chem Rev 1996, 96, 3201**, Sell's books (*Chemistry and the Sense of Smell*, Wiley 2014) and Buchbauer's musk/sandalwood QSAR papers were not fetched in this session. They are standard references (known from background knowledge) but their model contents are unverified here.
- No olfactophore models were found for **bitter almond (benzaldehyde/HCN)**, **urinous/androstenone** (receptor OR7D4 work exists, but no pharmacophore surfaced), **vetiver/woody** (beyond Kraft reviews), **fruity** or **spicy**. The 2000 *Angew* review reportedly covers fruity/green/spicy/woody, but its figures could not be read.

## Q2. Muguet and marine/Calone: key features and 2015–2026 updates

### Takeaway
**Muguet:** a single HBA osmophore (aldehyde carbonyl, or the OH/aldehyde of bifunctional odorants) with three hydrophobes plus excluded volumes. It was trained on 57 muguet aldehydes in Discovery Studio, and two of its hydrophobes coincide with the 1999 bifunctional model. Numerical distances were not found in any accessible source. **Marine:** an aromatic-ring binding site with an aliphatic hydrophobe at 6.3 Å from its centre (Kraft & Eichenberger 2003), plus the two ether oxygens and the ketone carbonyl of the benzodioxepinone. Since 2020 the model has been "revised" to accommodate benzodioxoles and benzodioxepines, and to reflect the discovery that some benzodioxepinones are actually muguet odorants.

### Cited Findings
- Muguet: the model uses "threshold data of a training set of **57 muguet aldehydes**" in Discovery Studio. It shows "which areas should denote hydrophobic substituents for van-der-Waals interaction… relative to the most polar functional group (osmophore, in this case a hydrogen-bond acceptor)" and where there would be excluded volumes. "Two of the three hydrophobes correspond almost exactly" to the 1999 bifunctional-muguet model. — [P&F, Discovery of Nympheal](https://www.perfumerflavorist.com/fragrance/ingredients/article/21856694/discovery-of-nympheal-the-definitive-muguet-aldehyde) (via search snippets; direct fetch blocked)
- Muguet update 2023: Lilybelle and Biomuguet are "currently the only renewable and biodegradable non-spermotoxic Lilial replacers" but lack Lilial's green-aquatic facets. Four hydrogenated Nympheal derivatives were made from an isobutyl Hagemann's ester and "rationalized by computational olfactophore models". — [Ohrmann et al. Helv Chim Acta 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/hlca.202300040)
- Muguet/marine crossover 2018: 6-isopropyl- and 6-isobutyl-benzodioxepinones smell of lily of the valley rather than marine. The 6-cyclopropyl analogue has th 0.065 ng/L air (in Lilial's range), and the 6-(cyclopropylmethyl) analogue resembles Bourgeonal, "rationalized by a superposition analysis". — [Jordi & Kraft 2018](https://doi.org/10.1002/hlca.201800048)
- Muguet receptor side 2017: C/Si/Ge/Sn switch analogues of Lilial/Bourgeonal were docked into an hOR17-4 (OR1D2) homology model. The paper argues for shape-based recognition in vivo. — [Wolf et al. PLoS ONE 2017](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0182147)
- 2020 review: the Lilial/Lyral replacement "kept the industry busy… with a plethora of new 'muguets'". — [Armanino et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202005719)
- Marine 2003: "an olfactophore model… indicates an aliphatic hydrophobe at a distance of 6.3 Å from the centre of the aromatic-ring binding site"; discovery of 7-(3′-methylbutyl)benzo[b][1,4]dioxepin-3-one as an extremely powerful marine odorant. — [Kraft & Eichenberger 2003](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200300174)
- Marine feature set (secondary): aromatic π-system + two H-bond-accepting ether oxygens + polarized carbonyl + hydrophobic alkyl group at defined distances. — [search summary of marine literature; see Riad et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/chemistry3040103)
- Marine, Phase model: a "Calone 1951 olfactophore model was produced using the phase program (version 3.4, Schrödinger…2012), and led to the identification of four structural features". Hydrophobic alkyl at positions 7/8 is optimal, C3–C6. Unsaturation, especially conjugated, lowers thresholds, and branching raises them. — [Riad et al., Chemistry 2021 (CC BY)](https://doi.org/10.3390/chemistry3040103)
- Marine "adapted" model: "two oxygen functions and an aromatic ring, one but not more than C3–C6 aliphatic substituent on this ring, and the absence of substituents in the α-position to the carbonyl"; the ring geometry "imposes the spatial orientation of the carbonyl group". — [Riad et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/chemistry3040103)
- Marine 2020: new benzodioxepines and benzodioxoles "required a revision of the existing olfactophore models". — [Armanino et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202005719)
- Marine RMIT view: a 7-alkyl group is essential, and the hydrophobic pocket adjacent to the aromatic-ring-binding site is "pivotal" for potency. — [Hügel et al. 2008](https://doi.org/10.1002/cbdv.200890083). Saturated (non-aromatic) analogues were used to test the aromatic-ring binding motif. — [Plummer et al. EurJOC 2014](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.201403142)
- Kraft/Popaj: "'Vanilla Oceanics': Synthesis and Olfactory Properties of (1′E)-7-(Prop-1′-enyl)-2H-benzo[b][1,4]dioxepin-3(4H)-ones", Synthesis 2010 (Kraft, Popaj, Müller, Schär). Closed. — [doi:10.1055/s-0030-1258142](https://api.openalex.org/works?search=olfactophore&per-page=100&select=title,publication_year,doi,authorships,primary_location,open_access)

### Inferences
- The marine and muguet olfactophores overlap: a benzodioxepinone whose alkyl group moves from C7 to C6 flips from marine to muguet. So the two models plausibly share an HBA + aromatic/hydrophobe core and differ mainly in where the hydrophobe sits relative to the osmophore.
- Numerical muguet geometry (HBA–hydrophobe distances) has apparently never been printed in text. Accessible sources give only the feature count (1 HBA + 3 Hy + excluded volumes) and the training-set size.

### Gaps
- No "Kraft & Popaj 2008 'Scent of the Sea' marine olfactophore" paper was confirmed. The Kraft/Popaj marine output found is Synthesis 2010. Titles such as "The Scent of the Sea" could not be verified.
- The full feature list and distances of the Phase Calone model (Riad Fig. 3) are figure-only, and its primary source is unclear.
- No dsm-firmenich or Symrise muguet olfactophore papers (post-2022 Lilial ban) were found beyond the Kraft/Ohrmann work. Industry models are probably unpublished or patent-only.

## Q3. Are any models available as downloadable pharmacophore files (.phar, .ph4, .pml, Phase .phypo, Catalyst .chm)?

### Takeaway
None found. No olfactophore model has been released as a machine-readable pharmacophore file in any supplement or repository located. Models are published as figures, sometimes with partial inter-feature distances (musk 2016) or a full training-set activity table and fit statistics (rose 2020), which would allow re-derivation but not direct import.

### Cited Findings
- Wolf et al. 2017 (muguet, PLoS ONE, CC BY) is the only item found with 3D structural supplementary files: docking poses as PyMOL .pse and Gaussian .log/QM outputs. These are receptor-docking data, not a pharmacophore hypothesis. — [PLoS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0182147)
- The rose nitrile model (Chimia 2020, CC BY-NC 4.0) publishes feature types (1 HBA, 2 Hy, 1 double-bond), fit statistics (r = 77.1%, total cost 68.9, RMS 1.87, uncert 3), software versions and a 12-compound training table with experimental and calculated activities. This is enough to re-build the model, but no coordinates are given. — [Hauser, Kraft, Carreira 2020](https://www.chimia.ch/chimia/article/download/2020_247/345/11000)
- The musk model (EurJOC 2016, closed) gives distances in the text: HBA–HBA 6.96 Å, Hy–Hy 7.51 Å, one Hy 0.91 Å in front of an HBA, and 4 excluded volumes. — [Liu et al. 2016 (copy)](https://pdfcoffee.com/synthesis-and-olfactory-properties-of-silicon-containing-analogs-of-rosamusk-romandolide-and-applelide-insights-into-the-structural-parameters-of-linear-alicyclic-musks-pdf-free.html)
- The marine 2003 model publishes one distance in its abstract (Hy at 6.3 Å from the aromatic-ring centroid). — [Kraft & Eichenberger 2003](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.200300174)
- The Gorbachov 1999 ETM ambergris fragment is defined by key distances and charges given in the (bronze OA) paper. It is a rule set, not a software file. — [Chem Senses 1999](https://doi.org/10.1093/chemse/24.2.171)
- Open-licence figures: Riad 2021 Calone Phase figure (CC BY 4.0); Chapado et al. 2009 sandalwood three-subunit description (CC BY 3.0) — [Riad](https://doi.org/10.3390/chemistry3040103); [Molecules 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6255316/)

### Inferences
- Most models come from industrial labs (Givaudan, Firmenich, Symrise) and appear in paywalled Wiley journals. The lack of files is probably deliberate: the models support captive-odorant design and patents.
- A practical route to a machine-readable "olfactophore database" would be to re-create the models from published training sets and thresholds. The rose (Chimia 2020) and musk (EurJOC 2016) papers are the best-specified candidates. Such re-creations would be new work, not published models.

### Gaps
- Wiley supporting-information files for Ohrmann 2023, Jordi 2018, Liu 2016 and Kuhnt 2025 could not be inspected (403). They may contain training-set structures or thresholds, but a pharmacophore file is unlikely given the field's practice. Unverified.
- No public repository (Zenodo, GitHub, PharmIt, ZINC-pharmacophore sets) holding odorant olfactophores was found. A targeted repository search was not run.

## Q4. Consolidated compilations or reviews listing olfactophores across families

### Takeaway
No single database exists. The closest consolidated sources are the two Angewandte reviews (Kraft et al. 2000; Armanino … Kraft 2020), organised by odor family with olfactophore figures; the book *Scent and Chemistry* (2nd ed. 2022), with a dedicated §3.10 "QSAR: Homology Versus Olfactophore Models"; Kraft's Chem Biodivers reviews (2004 musk, 2014 molecular parameters); and, for older rule-based models, Rossiter's 1996 Chem Rev (not verified here).

### Cited Findings
- Kraft et al. 2000 covers olfactophore models for fruity, marine, green, floral, spicy, woody, amber and musky. — [Angew 2000 record](https://api.openalex.org/works?search=olfactophore&per-page=100&select=title,publication_year,doi,authorships,primary_location,open_access)
- Armanino et al. 2020 is the same family structure 20 years on, including the marine olfactophore revision and muguet replacements. — [doi:10.1002/anie.202005719](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202005719)
- *Scent and Chemistry* 2nd ed. (Ohloff, Pickenhagen, Kraft, Grau; Wiley-VCH 2022): §3.9 bifunctional muguet units; §3.10 QSAR: homology vs olfactophore models; §8.1–8.4 musk and ambergris. — [TOC sample](https://content.e-bookshelf.de/media/reading/L-18511499-85192109e6.pdf)
- Riad et al. 2021 (CC BY) is an open-access compilation of marine SAR and olfactophore models. — [Chemistry 2021, 3, 1437](https://doi.org/10.3390/chemistry3040103)
- Doty 2025 is a history of odor classification and of attempts to map percepts to chemical parameters (hybrid OA). — [Chem Senses 2025](https://doi.org/10.1093/chemse/bjaf022)
- The Chimia 2020 rose paper has an open-access overview of olfactophore concepts: "olfactophore models visualize osmophoric groups and hydrophobic moieties in their three-dimensional arrangement", and there is "still no general olfactophore model for rose odorants", with Boelens' empirical rose rules as the baseline. — [Chimia 2020](https://www.chimia.ch/chimia/article/download/2020_247/345/11000)

### Inferences
- A complete catalogue has to be assembled from the Angewandte 2000/2020 reviews plus family-specific primary papers, as in the Q1 table above. The 2000 review is likely the richest single source of older Givaudan (Catalyst) olfactophore figures, but it is closed access.

### Gaps
- Sell, *Chemistry and the Sense of Smell* (Wiley 2014), Sell (ed.) *The Chemistry of Fragrances* (RSC, 2nd ed. 2006), Rossiter 1996 Chem Rev and Perfumer & Flavorist SOR articles were not accessed. Their olfactophore content is unverified.
- Buchbauer group (Vienna) QSAR/olfactophore studies of sandalwood and musk were not located beyond the 2009 Molecules paper. Zakarya's later papers (after the 1990 EJMC paper) were not found in OpenAlex searches.
