# Standards governing the construction and operation of an olfactometer (DIN / EN / ISO / VDI / ASTM)

Research status: September 2026. Standard texts are paywalled, so the numbers below come from public previews (iTeh sample PDFs), government reports, papers, and vendor or calibration-lab documents that cite the standards. Each number is tagged with its source. Numbers I could not confirm from a primary or near-primary source are marked **(uncertain)**.

## Q1. What is "the DIN norm for an olfactometer"? DIN EN 13725: edition, title, predecessor, price, previews

### Takeaway
The standard is **DIN EN 13725:2022-06**, the German adoption of EN 13725:2022, titled "Emissionen aus stationären Quellen – Bestimmung der Geruchsstoffkonzentration durch dynamische Olfaktometrie und die Geruchsstoffemissionsrate". It replaces DIN EN 13725:2003-07 and its 2006 corrigendum. The German PDF costs €245.50 and the English translation €307.10 from DIN Media. A free 15-page iTeh preview gives the table of contents, the scope and the definitions, but none of the numeric instrument clauses.

### Inferences
- Before 2022, German labs in effect worked from VDI 3881 (Parts 1–4, 1980/1989), then DIN EN 13725:2003, then DIN EN 13725:2022. When the user says "DIN norm for building an olfactometer", DIN EN 13725:2022-06 is the only current answer. No separate DIN standard covers olfactometer construction. The construction requirements are performance criteria (materials, dilution accuracy and stability, presentation flow) in clauses 6.5 and 7.4.

## Q2. What EN 13725 requires of the instrument

### Takeaway
EN 13725 specifies performance criteria, not a design. The main ones:
- **Dilution apparatus:** instrumental accuracy A_d ≤ 0.20 and instability I_d ≤ 5 % at every dilution step. These are verified at least once a year with a tracer gas such as CO or propane, measured by a calibrated analyser with expanded uncertainty below 3 %.
- **Presentation:** at least 20 L/min at the sniffing port. A secondary source adds a face velocity of at least 0.2 m/s (uncertain).
- **Dilution series:** step factor about 2 in practice.
- **Panel:** n-butanol individual thresholds of 20–80 ppb (62–246 µg/m³), with the antilog of the log standard deviation below 2.3.
- **Whole laboratory:** accuracy A_od ≤ 0.217 and repeatability r ≤ 0.477.
- **Materials:** only low-adsorption, odourless materials (PTFE, FEP, PET/Nalophan, PVF/Tedlar, passivated stainless steel). Glass was removed from the sample-container list in 2022.
- **Room:** the 2022 edition sets a temperature set-point above 21 °C and relative humidity of 20–80 %.

### Cited Findings

**Scope and definitions relevant to a threshold-measuring instrument (EN 13725:2022, clauses 1 and 3)**
- The scope explicitly lists "the measurement of the mass concentration at the detection threshold of odorants in g/m³" and "the determination of the SROM value of secondary reference odorant gas". The standard covers "mixtures of odorants of defined composition and undefined mixtures of odorants in air or nitrogen". — [iTeh preview, clause 1](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- The analysis step "may be fully applied in many cases not related with industrial emission sources", for example to "the measurement of the mass concentration at the detection threshold of odorant substances". In those cases, the requirements on measurement planning and stationary-source sampling "may be ignored or adapted". — [iTeh preview, clause 1](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- The following are explicitly excluded from scope:
  - perceived intensity above threshold
  - hedonic tone
  - field and direct olfactometry
  - static olfactometry
  - recognition (identification) threshold
  - ambient field panels, which EN 16841-1 covers

  — [iTeh preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- The typical measuring range is 10¹–10⁷ ouE/m³, including pre-dilution. — [iTeh preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- **EROM:** 1 European Reference Odour Mass = 123 µg n-butanol (CAS 71-36-3). Evaporated in 1 m³ of neutral gas, this gives 0.040 µmol/mol (40 ppb), which is 1 ouE/m³ by definition. — [iTeh preview, 3.1.13](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- A **dynamic olfactometer** is an "apparatus that delivers a flow of mixtures of odorous and neutral gas with known dilution factors in a common outlet". **Dynamic dilution** means mixing two known flows; the dilution factor can be determined from the flow rates. — [iTeh preview, 3.1.9–3.1.10](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- **Neutral gas** is air or nitrogen treated to be "as odourless as technically possible" that does not interfere with the odour according to panel members. — [iTeh preview, 3.1.23](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- **Measuring range of an olfactometer:** the theoretical range runs from the minimum dilution factor × step factor^1.5 to the maximum dilution factor ÷ step factor^1.5. The standard also defines "minimum dilution factor" and "maximum dilution factor" (lowest and highest settable) and "step factor". — [iTeh preview, 3.1.17–3.1.22, 3.1.55](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- The standard defines both a **forced-choice method** (the response "no difference" is not permitted, per EN ISO 5492) and a **yes/no method**. — [iTeh preview, 3.1.14, 3.1.58](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- EN 13725 recognises three presentation modes: triangular forced choice, binary forced choice, and yes/no. — [Scentroid WikiOdour EN13725](https://scentroid.com/wikiodour/en13725/)

**Dilution apparatus: calibration, accuracy and instability (clauses 6.5 and 7.4; Annex B example)**
- Dilution equipment must be calibrated "at least once a year" with a suitable tracer material such as carbon monoxide, using a physico-chemical analyser such as continuous infrared. Both the reference gas and the analyser must have an expanded uncertainty below 3 %. — [Trescal CIM2023 poster, "Calibration of Olfactometers according to EN 13725:2022"](https://www.trescal.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CIM2023-Trescal-POSTER-REF-190-Calibration-of-Olfactometers-1.pdf)
- For **each dilution** the equipment offers, accuracy and instability are determined. The acceptance criteria are **A_d < 0.20 (accuracy)** and **I_d < 5 % (instability)**. — [Trescal poster](https://www.trescal.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CIM2023-Trescal-POSTER-REF-190-Calibration-of-Olfactometers-1.pdf)
- The procedure: fill bags with CO reference mixtures, connect them to the olfactometer with air as diluent, place a calibrated CO analyser at an olfactometer outlet, and compare measured with theoretical dilution. Trescal Spain holds ENAC accreditation for this. Its accredited dilution range is up to 1:100,000. — [Trescal poster](https://www.trescal.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CIM2023-Trescal-POSTER-REF-190-Calibration-of-Olfactometers-1.pdf)
- The UBA/Olfasense study calibrated its FID with propane in N₂ (90 ppm) before each use. For acetone checks, the FID probe sat in the centre of the olfactometer's sniffing funnel, "to best represent the nose position", and readings were averaged over 60 s. — [UBA Texte Umwelt & Gesundheit 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)
- The 2022 revision changed pre-dilution: dynamic pre-dilution during sampling now needs the dilution factor determined in the sample itself, from flow measurements or indicator-gas concentrations, instead of relying on instrument calibration alone. — [Olores.org, what is new in EN13725:2021](https://www.olores.org/en/techniques/dynamic-olfactometry/1210-what-is-new-in-the-revised-en13725-2021-standard); indicator example (oxygen with O₂-free N₂ diluent) in [search summary of ResearchGate "Update on revised EN 13725"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/351942376_Update_on_the_revised_EN_137252021)
- Numbering discrepancy: the Trescal poster cites "section 6.5.5 and Annex C" and "section 5.4.2" for calibration. The 2022 table of contents puts the dilution-apparatus quality requirements in 7.4, the accuracy/instability example in Annex B, and 5.4 does not exist. The poster seems to mix clause numbers from different drafts or editions. — [Trescal poster](https://www.trescal.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/CIM2023-Trescal-POSTER-REF-190-Calibration-of-Olfactometers-1.pdf) vs [iTeh TOC](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)

**Presentation (sniffing port) flow**
- EN 13725:2003 set a flow of 20 L/min at each sniffing port. Lower flows let the assessor inhale ambient air, which dilutes the sample and raises the apparent threshold.
  - Bokowa (2006) found flows below 14 L/min significantly biased results.
  - 3 L/min, typical of 1980s olfactometers, gave results up to 10× lower than 20 L/min.
  - Bokowa therefore treats pre-2003 threshold compilations as needing update.

  — [Bokowa 2022, CET 95:211–216](https://www.cetjournal.it/cet/22/95/036.pdf)
- German Wikipedia summarises the standard's rule as: air flow to assessors "darf nicht unter 20 l/min liegen", with face velocity at the outlet "mindestens 0,2 m/s". This is a secondary source; the 0.2 m/s figure is **(uncertain)**. — [de.wikipedia Olfaktometrie](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfaktometrie)
- Scentroid also lists 20 L/min at the presentation port, plus unspecified requirements on presentation airspeed, minimum and maximum dilution, and maximum step size. — [Scentroid WikiOdour](https://scentroid.com/wikiodour/en13725/)
- One search summary gives a range of 16–64 L/min depending on sniffing-port design. I could not trace this to a primary source, so it is **(uncertain)**. — [search result summary; no primary source located]

**Dilution range and step factor**
- German Wikipedia says the dilution unit must cover "zumindest den Verdünnungsbereich von 1:128 bis 1:16384", with "ein minimaler Bereich von 2^13 (13 Verdünnungsstufen)". It adds that commercial devices span 1:4 to 1:65,536 (2^14). This is **internally inconsistent**, because 1:128 to 1:16,384 is only 2^7. Treat the exact range rule as **(uncertain)** until checked in clause 6.5. — [de.wikipedia Olfaktometrie](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfaktometrie)
- In practice labs use a step factor of about 2. The UBA/Olfasense study notes dilution falls by roughly a factor of 2 per step. Its TO8 olfactometer covers about 1:4 to 1:65,000 in 15 steps. — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)
- A step-factor limit of "1.4 to 2.4" is often quoted from memory. I found **no public source** confirming it, so it is **(uncertain)**. Clause 6.5 of the purchased standard must be checked.

**Materials in contact with the sample**
- 2022 changes to sample-container and wetted materials:
  - **Glass removed** from the list.
  - Titanium and aluminium **added**, for sampling hoods and wind tunnels only.
  - Stainless steel must now avoid inadequate recovery, "e.g. passivated stainless steel", especially for H₂S and reduced sulphur compounds.
  - New leak and integrity tests.
  - A bag-conditioning procedure.
  - Maximum sample storage time stays at 30 h.

  — [Olores.org](https://www.olores.org/en/techniques/dynamic-olfactometry/1210-what-is-new-in-the-revised-en13725-2021-standard)
- LOD Srl confirms glass is "not allowed" in 2022 and rubber remains prohibited. It also reports new leak testing of bags, integrity testing of sampling lines, and one "blank" sample per year per instrument type. — [LOD Srl, UNI EN 13725:2022 changes](https://www.lodsrl.it/en/news/uni-en-13725-2022-how-did-the-standard-change)
  - Caution: LOD says previously permitted aluminium, titanium and stainless steel are "no longer listed". That conflicts with Olores.org, which says they were added or retained with conditions. Olores.org is written by the standard's working-group community and seems more reliable.
- Common bag films are Nalophan (PET), Tedlar (PVF) and PTFE. — [Scentroid WikiOdour](https://scentroid.com/wikiodour/en13725/)
- Bag material matters for recovery. The UBA study notes losses in Nalophan bags for some substances and recommends correcting thresholds for low recovery. — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)

**Panel, laboratory performance and room**
- Panel selection with n-butanol:
  - Individual n-butanol threshold must be in **20–80 ppb**.
  - Individual log standard deviation, expressed as 10^s, must be **below 2.3**.
  - At least **10 individual threshold estimates (ITEs)** of n-butanol are needed for the calculation.

  — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf); also [Iowa DNR / St. Croix Sensory review](https://www.iowadnr.gov/media/2318/download?inline=) ("average n-butanol odor threshold of 20-80ppb and log standard deviation of <2.3")
- 2022 changes to panel monitoring:
  - The running monitoring sample is fixed at **n = 12 ITEs**, up from 10.
  - ITE data used for qualification may be at most **12 months** old.
  - Secondary reference odorants with known SROM values are allowed.

  — [Olores.org](https://www.olores.org/en/techniques/dynamic-olfactometry/1210-what-is-new-in-the-revised-en13725-2021-standard)
- Laboratory quality criteria on n-butanol, under repeatability conditions:
  - accuracy **A_od ≤ 0.217**
  - repeatability **r ≤ 0.477**, meaning two consecutive results differ by no more than about a factor of 3 in 95 % of cases

  — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf); [WST 2010 panel-selection paper (search summary)](https://doi.org/10.2166/wst.2010.023)
- Per measurement:
  - at least **8 ITEs** are needed
  - ITEs deviating from the mean by more than a factor **|ΔZ| > 5** are removed (retrospective screening)
  - usually 4 panellists are used

  — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)
- The minimum panel size is 4. — [Scentroid WikiOdour](https://scentroid.com/wikiodour/en13725/)
- The ITE is the geometric mean of the last dilution not detected and the first dilution detected consistently. The panel threshold is the geometric mean of the ITEs. — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)
- Room conditions in 2022:
  - Temperature set-point **> 21 °C**; the temperature must not fall more than 2 °C below the set-point.
  - Relative humidity **20–80 %**.
  - The old recommendation of 25 °C maximum was dropped.

  — [LOD Srl](https://www.lodsrl.it/en/news/uni-en-13725-2022-how-did-the-standard-change); [Olores.org](https://www.olores.org/en/techniques/dynamic-olfactometry/1210-what-is-new-in-the-revised-en13725-2021-standard)
- CO₂ in the sniffing room must not exceed 0.15 % (1500 ppm), per a secondary source. Which edition it comes from is **(uncertain)**. — [de.wikipedia Olfaktometrie](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfaktometrie)
- The 2022 edition adds explicit measurement uncertainty per EN ISO 20988 (10.2) and LoD/LoQ (10.3), replacing the former "decision limit". It also adds health-and-safety requirements for assessors (9.5). — [Olores.org](https://www.olores.org/en/techniques/dynamic-olfactometry/1210-what-is-new-in-the-revised-en13725-2021-standard); [iTeh TOC](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- Assessor safety in practice: the UBA/Olfasense study prepared samples so that the **lowest settable dilution** stayed within German occupational exposure limits (AGW, TRGS 900). Where no AGW existed, it used GESTIS international limits. — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a self-built instrument, the hard quantitative targets that can be checked without the standard are:
  - A_d ≤ 0.20 and I_d ≤ 5 % at every dilution step, checked with a tracer gas (CO by NDIR, propane by FID, or a PID-detectable VOC)
  - at least 20 L/min at the port
  - a step factor of about 2
  - panel and lab criteria on n-butanol: 20–80 ppb, 10^s < 2.3, A_od ≤ 0.217, r ≤ 0.477
- Designers should buy DIN EN 13725:2022 for the exact wording of clauses 6.1, 6.5, 6.6 and 7.4, and of Annex B, where the formula for A_d and I_d is set out.
- n-butanol is both the panel reference and the unit definition (40 ppb = 1 ouE/m³). A self-built instrument should therefore be validated by measuring the n-butanol threshold of a qualified panel and landing in the 20–80 ppb window.

### Gaps
- Exact wording and values of clause 6.5 (minimum and maximum dilution, step-factor limits, face velocity, rinse and presentation times, interval between presentations) could not be verified from free sources. The 2^13 range, the 1.4–2.4 step factor and the 0.2 m/s face velocity all need checking.
- The exact formulas for A_d and I_d, and the number of tracer replicates per dilution step, are not public. They are in 7.4 and Annex B.
- The presentation-time limits for yes/no versus forced-choice modes (e.g. about 2–3 s stimulus, flushing between presentations) were not found in citable form.
- n-butanol ppb ↔ µg/m³ conversion: 20–80 ppb corresponds to about 62–246 µg/m³ at 20 °C (40 ppb = 123 µg/m³). This was computed, not quoted.

## Q3. VDI guidelines: sampling, intensity, hedonic tone, implementation guidance

### Takeaway
The most relevant VDI documents are:
- **VDI 3884 Blatt 1 (2015-02):** German supplementary instructions for applying DIN EN 13725. A revision for the 2022 edition is a project planned for May 2027.
- **VDI 3882 Blatt 1 (1992-10):** odour intensity. The 1992 edition is still valid ("überprüft und bestätigt"); a revision draft appeared in November 2021.
- **VDI 3882 Blatt 2:** hedonic tone.
- **VDI 3940 Blatt 4:** hedonic polarity profiles.
- **VDI 3880:** static sampling.
- **VDI 3881 Parts 1–4:** the pre-EN German olfactometry guideline, superseded by EN 13725.

For intensity above threshold, use VDI 3882-1, because EN 13725 explicitly excludes intensity.

### Cited Findings
- **VDI 3884 Blatt 1**, "Olfaktometrie – Bestimmung der Geruchsstoffkonzentration mit dynamischer Olfaktometrie – Ausführungshinweise zur Norm DIN EN 13725" (2015-02).
  - It supplements DIN EN 13725 and sets further practical requirements where they matter for result quality, because the standard left "interpretive discretion".
  - It supports uniform application and accreditation to DIN EN ISO/IEC 17025.
  - It describes measurement uncertainty based on the laboratory's daily n-butanol standard deviation.

  — [VDI](https://www.vdi.de/richtlinien/details/vdi-3884-blatt-1-olfactometry-determination-of-odour-concentration-by-dynamic-olfactometry-supplementary-instructions-for-application-of-din-en-13725); [DIN Media 2015-02](https://www.dinmedia.de/en/technical-rule/vdi-3884-blatt-1/224206973)
- VDI 3884 Blatt 1 is being revised for DIN EN 13725:2022. VDI lists it as a "Projekt" with planned publication in **May 2027**. — [VDI page](https://www.vdi.de/richtlinien/details/vdi-3884-blatt-1-olfaktometrie-bestimmung-der-geruchsstoffkonzentration-mit-dynamischer-olfaktometrie-ausfuehrungshinweise-zur-norm-din-en-13725)
- **VDI-EE 3884 Blatt 1.1** gives recommendations on determining measurement uncertainty for olfactometry per DIN EN 13725. — [VDI](https://www.vdi.de/mitgliedschaft/vdi-richtlinien/details/vdi-ee-3884-blatt-11-olfaktometrie-empfehlungen-und-ausfuehrungshinweise-zur-bestimmung-der-messunsicherheit-der-olfaktometrie-nach-din-en-13725)
- **VDI 3882 Blatt 1**, "Olfaktometrie; Bestimmung der Geruchsintensität".
  - Issued October 1992, 27 pages, €96.60, in German and English.
  - Status "Überprüft und bestätigt"; responsible body is KRdL.
  - Scope: odour samples above threshold, rated by sensation intensity in humans.

  — [VDI page](https://www.vdi.de/richtlinien/details/vdi-3882-blatt-1-olfaktometrie-bestimmung-der-geruchsintensitaet)
- A **draft VDI 3882 Blatt 1:2021-11** exists at €71.70 for the PDF. It applies to emissions, **single substances or defined mixtures** in neutral air. — [DIN Media draft](https://www.dinmedia.de/en/draft-technical-rule/vdi-3882-blatt-1/338076832)
- How the UBA/Olfasense study applied VDI 3882-1 intensity:
  - Constant-stimuli method with concentrations in random order.
  - Each concentration presented 3× alternating with neutral air.
  - 7-point category scale: 0 nicht wahrnehmbar, 1 sehr schwach, 2 schwach, 3 deutlich, 4 stark, 5 sehr stark, 6 extrem stark.
  - The dilution at which 50 % of responses exceed each level is plotted against log dilution. The slope is the Weber–Fechner coefficient k_W, giving I = k_W · log(c / c_threshold).

  — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)
- **VDI 3882 Blatt 2** covers hedonic tone. — [VDI](https://www.vdi.de/richtlinien/details/vdi-3882-blatt-2-olfaktometrie-bestimmung-der-hedonischen-geruchswirkung-1)
- **VDI 3940 Blatt 4** covers hedonic tone via polarity profiles ("Duft" vs "Gestank"). UBA used it for 20 single substances. — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)
- **VDI 3881 Parts 1–4** (drafted 1980, revised 1989) was the German olfactometry standard before the CEN standard. Its definitions include the "reaction limit" at 16 % detection. — [Iowa DNR / St. Croix review](https://www.iowadnr.gov/media/2318/download?inline=)
- **VDI 3880** covers static sampling; German Wikipedia lists it together with VDI 3884. — [de.wikipedia](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfaktometrie)
- **Precedent for pure compounds:** UBA's 2023 report (Olfasense, Kiel) measured odour thresholds, intensity and hedonic tone of **20 pure indoor pollutants**.
  - Thresholds followed DIN EN 13725:2003 on a TO8s olfactometer.
  - Intensity followed VDI 3882-1; hedonic tone followed VDI 3940-4.
  - 12 panellists per parameter.
  - Pure substances were vaporised with a Liquid-to-Gas Vaporiser (LiGaVa) into Nalophan "mother bags"; solids were first dissolved in an odourless solvent.
  - Concentrations were checked by FID, GC-MS and GC-IMS.

  — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)

### Inferences
- If the ML dataset also needs intensity, the German-accepted pairing is EN 13725 for thresholds plus VDI 3882-1 for intensity and the Weber–Fechner slope. It runs on the same dynamic olfactometer, which is a strong argument for a design that can present a randomised sequence of fixed dilutions, not only ascending series.
- The UBA report is the closest methodological template for the user's goal (single molecules, German context), and it is free.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether the VDI 3882-1 2021 draft became a final edition by 2026. VDI still shows the 1992 edition as valid.
- I did not verify the current status of VDI 3882 Blatt 2 or VDI 3881 (probably withdrawn), or the exact edition of VDI 3880.

## Q4. Sensory-science standards for pure-compound thresholds (ISO 13301, ASTM E679 / E1432 / E544, ISO 5496 / 8586 / 8589, ISO 16000-28 / 30)

### Takeaway
For research thresholds of pure molecules, the sensory-science standards are **ISO 13301:2018** (3-AFC data collection and logistic/maximum-likelihood fitting), **ASTM E679-19** (3-AFC ascending method of limits with a best-estimate threshold) and **ASTM E1432-19** (individual and group thresholds from intermediate-size forced-choice data). They put few requirements on the instrument beyond inert materials, a homogeneous medium and constant step factors. ISO 13301 says outright that it does not address air-quality standardisation under EN 13725.

### Cited Findings

**ISO 13301:2018**, "Sensory analysis — Methodology — General guidance for measuring odour, flavour and taste detection thresholds by a three-alternative forced-choice (3-AFC) procedure", 2nd edition, 2018-04
- Background:
  - Replaces ISO 13301:2002.
  - Prepared by ISO/TC 34/SC 12.
  - Its only normative reference is ISO 5492.

  — [ISO 13301 iTeh preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf); [ISO catalogue](https://www.iso.org/standard/68901.html)
- Scope:
  - Includes studying "the ability of a chemical substance to stimulate the chemoreceptive senses" and "the relationship of molecular structure to capacity of a chemical to act as a stimulant".
  - Does not cover recognition thresholds or air-quality standardisation "as discussed in EN 13725".

  — [ISO 13301 preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf)
- Definitions:
  - Detection threshold is the stimulus with probability of detection 0.5 under the threshold model.
  - p_d = 1.5 × p_c − 0.5, so the threshold is where p_c = 2/3.
  - The logistic model is fitted by maximum likelihood; the best estimate threshold (BET) is covered in 6.6.
  - Annex A gives the number of assessors needed for a given precision.

  — [ISO 13301 preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf)
- Sample and instrument requirements (5.1.2 Gases):
  - Prepare stimulus and medium in vessels "such as polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) coated bottles or balloons".
  - Flush vessels and tubing repeatedly with fresh sample to saturate the walls.
  - Keep a constant temperature.
  - Use "smoothbore PTFE-coated tubing and valves free from points of sudden pressure change".
  - Air is the expected gaseous medium.
  - Prepare samples in a facility conforming to ISO 8589, in containers that neither adsorb nor contribute odour.
  - The presence of the stimulus must not be detectable by any cue other than the chemical senses.

  — [ISO 13301 preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf)
- Concentration steps (5.2):
  - Constant factor X of about 3–5 for approximate studies and about 2 for higher precision.
  - The most informative points are 45–90 % correct (p_d 0.18–0.85).
  - Locate each assessor's range with a large X first, then tailor.

  — [ISO 13301 preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf)
- Liquids: prepare dilutions directly from stock by microsyringe instead of serially, because adsorption losses grow at higher dilution. Purify solvents with activated carbon. — [ISO 13301 preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf)
- ISO 13301 notes that literature thresholds for one substance often "span two or three orders of magnitude or more", mostly from differing concepts and procedures. It also notes that thresholds decrease with repeated testing as assessors learn. — [ISO 13301 preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf)

**ASTM standards**
- **ASTM E679-19**, "Standard Practice for Determination of Odor and Taste Thresholds By a Forced-Choice Ascending Concentration Series Method of Limits". It prescribes 3-AFC with an ascending concentration series. — [ASTM](https://www.astm.org/Standards/E679.htm); [ScienceDirect 2012 on implementing E679](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0950329312000286)
  - Search summaries say it uses about 5–8 concentration steps with a constant factor (e.g. 3), and the individual threshold is the geometric mean of the last miss and first correct response. This matches common descriptions but was not read in the standard text, so it is **(secondary)**.
- **ASTM E1432-19**, reapproved **E1432-19(2025)**, covers individual and group thresholds from forced-choice data sets of intermediate size.
  - More than 20–40 3-AFC presentations per individual.
  - A group threshold from 5–15 individual thresholds.
  - A revision work item, WK90517, is open.

  — [ASTM store](https://store.astm.org/e1432-19.html); [WK90517](https://www.astm.org/workitem-wk90517); [Document Center](https://document-center.com/standards/show/ASTM-E1432)
- **ASTM E544-18**, "Standard Practice for Referencing Suprathreshold Odor Intensity".
  - Scale: n-butanol with a geometric ratio of 2.
  - Procedure A (dynamic scale): a dynamic-dilution apparatus with a series of sniffing ports delivering constant n-butanol concentrations at constant flow.
  - Procedure B: static scale.
  - Common static scales include an 8-point scale starting at 12 ppm butanol with ratio 2, and a 5-point scale starting at 25 ppm with ratio 3.

  — [ASTM E544-18](https://store.astm.org/e0544-18.html); [Iowa DNR / St. Croix review](https://www.iowadnr.gov/media/2318/download?inline=)

**ISO assessor and room standards**
- **ISO 8586:2023** (2nd edition, April 2023) covers selection and training of sensory assessors. It is also EN ISO 8586:2023. — [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/76667.html); [ANSI blog](https://blog.ansi.org/ansi/iso-8586-2023-training-of-sensory-assessors/)
- **ISO 8589:2007 + Amd 1:2014** gives test-room design guidance; the German version is DIN EN ISO 8589:2010. A revision, **prEN ISO 8589 / ISO/DIS 8589 (2025)**, is in progress. — [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/36385.html); [prEN ISO 8589](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/cen/ecca3991-3918-4da4-88f8-c52d7db8c21e/pren-iso-8589)
- **ISO 5496:2006 + Amd 1:2018** covers initiation and training of assessors in detecting and recognising odours. — [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standard/44247.html); [BS ISO 5496:2006+A1:2018](https://www.en-standard.eu/bs-iso-5496-2006-a1-2018-sensory-analysis-methodology-initiation-and-training-of-assessors-in-the-detection-and-recognition-of-odours/)

**Indoor-air odour standards**
- **ISO 16000-28:2020**, German version DIN ISO 16000-28:2021-11. It covers odour emissions from building products in test chambers, assessed by a sensory panel for acceptability and perceived intensity. Perceived intensity is rated against a comparison device (acetone, "pi" scale). — [VDI listing](https://www.vdi.de/en/home/vdi-standards/details/din-iso-16000-28-indoor-air-part-28-determination-of-odour-emissions-from-building-products-using-test-chambers-iso-16000-282020); [DIN Media](https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-iso-16000-28/344862394)
- Olfasense's Acetone Comparative Scale claims compliance with ISO 16000-28, ISO 16000-30 and VDI 4302 Part 1.
  - Pi scale 0–15, i.e. 16 concentrations.
  - Sample flow at the nose funnel 40 L/min (≈0.67 L/s).
  - Glass nose funnel.
  - Capillary-tube dosing with pressure regulation.

  — [Olfasense](https://www.olfasense.com/odour-measurement-equipment/instruments/acetone-comparative-scale/)

### Inferences
- ISO 13301 + ASTM E679/E1432 give the defensible psychophysics and statistics for per-molecule thresholds, including slope, confidence intervals and individual versus group values. EN 13725 gives the instrument QA vocabulary (A_d, I_d, 20 L/min, n-butanol panel check). A research olfactometer can reasonably follow both: EN 13725-style dilution calibration and n-butanol panel screening, plus ISO 13301 3-AFC presentation and logistic fitting.
- ISO 13301's advice to prepare each dilution directly from stock, and to watch adsorption at high dilution, applies directly to a dynamic-dilution build. Wetted paths should be PTFE or FEP, and cascaded dilution stages should be verified with a tracer.

### Gaps
- Full text of ASTM E679-19 (step sizes, number of assessors, sample presentation apparatus) was not read. Snippet-derived details are marked secondary.
- DIN national sensory standards (DIN 10959 threshold testing, DIN 10961 assessor training) were **not researched**; their status is unknown.
- ISO 16000-30 content and the numeric pi-scale definition (acetone mg/m³ per pi unit) were not confirmed from a public source.

## Q5. Which standard fits the user's goal (pure-compound thresholds for an ML dataset), and is EN 13725 conformity needed?

### Takeaway
No standard legally requires EN 13725 conformity for research. It is mandatory only where regulators or accreditation (DIN EN ISO/IEC 17025, §29b BImSchG labs) require odour-concentration results. For pure-compound thresholds, EN 13725:2022 explicitly lists "mass concentration at the detection threshold of odorants" in its scope and allows the sampling and planning parts to be ignored or adapted. It is therefore the best reference for instrument QA and cross-lab comparability. ISO 13301 and ASTM E679 are better suited for the psychophysical design and statistics.

### Cited Findings
- EN 13725:2022 scope includes "the measurement of the mass concentration at the detection threshold of odorants in g/m³". For such non-emission uses, its measurement-planning and sampling requirements "may be ignored or adapted". — [iTeh preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- EN 13725 excludes intensity, hedonic tone, recognition thresholds and static olfactometry. — [iTeh preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/66788/c998a012812c4d38a4f8f66cda87bd57/SIST-EN-13725-2022.pdf)
- ISO 13301 targets "investigation of the ability of a chemical substance to stimulate the chemoreceptive senses", including structure–activity studies. It explicitly does not address EN 13725 air-quality standardisation. — [ISO 13301 preview](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/68901/611b32a6fc864ad8b448f22273aaf801/ISO-13301-2018.pdf)
- VDI 3884-1 exists to support uniform application and **accreditation** under DIN EN ISO/IEC 17025. Its target users are testing bodies under §29b BImSchG, environmental labs and authorities. — [VDI 3884-1](https://www.vdi.de/richtlinien/details/vdi-3884-blatt-1-olfaktometrie-bestimmung-der-geruchsstoffkonzentration-mit-dynamischer-olfaktometrie-ausfuehrungshinweise-zur-norm-din-en-13725)
- Method choice changes threshold values substantially:
  - Sniffing-port flow below 20 L/min yields up to 10× higher (worse) thresholds.
  - Pre-2003 compilations (Leonardos 1967, Amoore & Hautala 1983, AIHA 1989, van Gemert) mix methods.

  — [Bokowa 2022 CET](https://www.cetjournal.it/cet/22/95/036.pdf)
- Bokowa measured 52 pure compounds per EN 13725:2003 and Ontario ON-6:
  - Compound injected by syringe into sample bags filled with N₂ (e.g. 100 µL hexane or 10 µL toluene into 20 L).
  - Mixed 30 min; evaluated within 2 h.
  - Binary-choice mode; screened panel.

  — [Bokowa 2022 CET](https://www.cetjournal.it/cet/22/95/036.pdf)
- Nagata (Japan) measured 223 substances by the triangle odor bag method with a 6-person panel. Primary samples were made in polyester bags with N₂. Recovery was poor for amines, fatty acids, indole and skatole (e.g. indole 6.5 %, butyric acid 40 %, trimethylamine 13–93 % depending on injector), so those thresholds were corrected. — [Nagata, Japan MoE](https://www.env.go.jp/en/air/odor/measure/02_3_2.pdf)
- UBA found analytically measured concentrations sometimes well below calculated ones because of Nalophan bag losses. It recommends correcting thresholds for recovery. — [UBA 04/2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/04_2023_ug_bestimmung_von_geruchswahrnehmungsschwellen.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Recommendation for the report writer.** Present EN 13725:2022 as the "DIN norm" the user asked about, and use it as the instrument-performance spec:
  - dilution accuracy and instability with tracer verification
  - at least 20 L/min at the port
  - inert wetted materials
  - neutral-air quality
  - n-butanol panel screening (20–80 ppb)

  Then recommend ISO 13301:2018 (and optionally ASTM E679-19/E1432-19) for the threshold protocol and statistics. Add VDI 3882-1 if intensity is wanted. Full EN 13725 conformity (accreditation, uncertainty budget per EN ISO 20988) is not needed for research, but reporting against its criteria makes the dataset comparable with the post-2003 literature.
- For ML-dataset quality, the biggest error sources are those that publications and standards stress: port flow, adsorption and recovery losses, and panel sensitivity. Each threshold record should store the analytically verified concentration (GC/PID/FID), the panel's n-butanol threshold on the day, and the step factor and presentation mode.

### Gaps
- No German or EU rule was found requiring EN 13725 for academic sensory research. The statement that it is "not legally needed" is an inference from scope and accreditation context, not from a cited legal text.
- Ethics and safety review for human exposure (LMU ethics committee, occupational exposure limits) was not researched. The UBA study used TRGS 900 AGW as the ceiling for the lowest dilution.

## Q6. International analogues (Japan, China, Australia/NZ, US)

### Takeaway
- **Japan:** triangle odor bag method (Ministry of the Environment Notification No. 63, 1995), with at least 6 panellists screened with 5 standard odorants (T&T olfactometer).
- **China:** HJ 1262-2022 replaced GB/T 14675-93 on 15 January 2023.
- **Australia:** AS EN 13725:2025 replaced AS/NZS 4323.3:2001; New Zealand still uses the 2001 standard.
- **US:** ASTM E679-19 / E1432-19 for thresholds and E544-18 for intensity.

### Cited Findings
- Japan: the Offensive Odor Control Law was amended in 1995. The triangle odor bag method was notified in 1995 (MoE Notification No. 63). The panel has 6 or more members who passed screening with 5 standard odorants (the T&T olfactometer set). Three bags per panellist, one containing the diluted sample; the test is repeated 3× per dilution. — [Japan MoE, Odor Index Regulation](https://www.env.go.jp/en/air/odor/regulation/); [Japan MoE, Olfactory measurement](https://www.env.go.jp/en/air/odor/olfactory_mm/); [Higuchi, Odor measurement in Japan](https://www.env.go.jp/content/900450121.pdf)
- Japan pure-compound dataset: 223 substances were measured 1976–1988 by the triangle odor bag method with a 6-person panel screened on the T&T olfactometer. Thresholds ranged from 0.77 ppt (isoamyl mercaptan) to 1500 ppm (propane). The spread for the same substance was about a factor of 5 at most. — [Nagata, Japan MoE PDF](https://www.env.go.jp/en/air/odor/measure/02_3_2.pdf)
- China: **HJ 1262-2022** "环境空气和废气 臭气的测定 三点比较式臭袋法" (ambient air and waste gas, triangle odor bag method). Released 14 July 2022, effective **15 January 2023**. It replaces GB/T 14675-93 (1993). — [MEE announcement](https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywgz/fgbz/bz/bzwb/jcffbz/202209/t20220909_993623.shtml); [CNEMC PDF](https://www.cnemc.cn/jcgf/dqhj/202212/P020221207618869438800.pdf)
- Australia and New Zealand: AS EN 13725:2025 (29 June 2025) replaced AS/NZS 4323.3:2001 in Australia, and older olfactometers may not meet the new requirements. New Zealand has not adopted it. — [Olores.org](https://www.olores.org/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=1469%3Aaustralia-fully-adopts-the-european-standard-with-as-en-13725-2025&catid=95&lang=en&idU=2&acm=_395)
- History: pre-CEN national standards included France AFNOR X-43-101 (1981/1986), Germany VDI 3881 Parts 1–4 (1980/1989) and the Netherlands NVN 2820 (1987/1995). prEN 13725 was released for comment in 1999. AS/NZS 4323.3:2001 adopted the CEN approach. — [Iowa DNR / St. Croix Sensory review](https://www.iowadnr.gov/media/2318/download?inline=)
- Triangle-bag and dynamic-olfactometry thresholds have been compared for the same odorants. — [ResearchGate table](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Thresholds-of-odorants-measured-by-triangle-odor-bag-method-and-dynamic-olfactometry-in_tbl1_24311851)

### Inferences
- The Japanese Nagata/triangle-bag dataset (223 compounds) and the EN 13725-based sets (Bokowa's 52 compounds, UBA's 20) are the main method-consistent pure-compound threshold sources. When pooling for ML, method should be a feature or stratification variable.

### Gaps
- Numeric details of HJ 1262-2022 (dilution factors, panel size, screening odorants) and the five Japanese standard odorants with their concentrations were not extracted.
- The ResearchGate triangle-bag vs dynamic-olfactometry comparison table was not opened.
