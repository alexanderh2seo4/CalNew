What this model means. This is a ligand-only reconstruction, not the original proprietary Phase model or a receptor simulation. Feature spheres are tolerance regions or visual markers for H-bond donors, H-bond acceptors, hydrophobic sites and aromatic rings. They are not electron density clouds. The fit score does not predict EC50 or perceived odor.

From structures to a calculated model

25 previously known B agonists form the training set. Isoeugenol (B3) seeds the alignment. Up to 48 conformers are generated per stereoisomer; converged minima within 6 kcal/mol of that molecule's lowest MMFF energy are retained. The 25 A compounds are screened independently against the resulting model.

Typed features come from RDKit's standard chemical feature definitions. One-to-one family-wise matching prevents reuse of a molecular feature. Candidate conformers are rigidly optimized against the consensus; their internal MMFF geometry is retained. Gaussian feature fit is weighted by training support. Missing feature contributions are zero; there is no compulsory donor rule.

Donor means a hydrogen-bond donor group, such as phenolic OH. Acceptor means a site that can accept a hydrogen bond using an electron pair. Donor-free compounds display no ligand donor marker when “Calculated ligand feature sites” is selected.

Calculated consensus features

FeatureSupportRMS spread ÅTolerance σ Å

Centers are the mean coordinates of matched training features. Tolerance σ = max(0.75 Å, 1.5 × RMS spread). The 0.75 Å floor, support cutoff (40%), initial matching cutoff (1.8 Å) and Gaussian scoring are explicit reconstruction settings, not values recovered from the original paper.

Consensus sphere radii equal σ. Individual ligand site spheres use a 0.45 Å display radius to mark calculated coordinates; that radius has no physical or score meaning. H-bond vectors, receptor exclusion volumes, docking, molecular dynamics, electrostatics and quantum electron densities are not calculated here.

Assay limits: 17 positives and 8 “no active response” compounds were preselected by the original multistage virtual screen. This small retrospective test is biased toward plausible ligands. It cannot establish prospective screening performance. High-scoring nonhits remain in the ranking.
